# Refund and Returns Policy
Version: 2026.1
Last updated: 2026-05-15
Doc type: policy
Product lines: all active retail products

## 1. Return window
Customers may request a standard return within 14 calendar days of delivery. The item must be in resalable condition. An unopened product with its original packaging is normally eligible when the return reason is covered by this policy.

Opened products may be returned within the same 14-day window only when they are defective on arrival or materially different from the ordered item. A change of mind after opening does not qualify for a standard change-of-mind return.

## 2. Shipping responsibility
For a verified defective-on-arrival item or an incorrect shipment, Pearlz arranges and pays return shipping. For an eligible unopened change-of-mind return, the customer pays return shipping unless the order promotion explicitly says otherwise.

## 3. Refund processing
Card payments are refunded to the original payment method after the returned item passes inspection. Allow up to 5 business days for Pearlz processing, followed by the payment provider's normal settlement period. Bank-transfer refunds are processed within 3 business days after approval. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded through the supported bank-transfer process after account verification.

## 4. Non-refundable categories
Used consumable filter cartridges are non-refundable. Custom installation services that have already been performed are non-refundable. Items excluded by a promotional bundle's stated conditions are also non-refundable.

## 5. Return vs replacement vs warranty repair
A return reverses an eligible purchase under this policy. A replacement exchanges an eligible item for another unit under the return rules. A warranty repair addresses a covered defect after the return window or when warranty terms are the appropriate route. These routes must not be conflated.

## 6. Promotional bundles
If a bundle is returned, all components supplied as part of the bundle must normally be returned together. A partial bundle return may result in the promotional discount being recalculated under the offer terms. The policy does not guarantee a partial-bundle refund.

## 7. Restocking
No standard restocking fee is charged for defective-on-arrival or incorrect shipments. A 10% restocking fee may apply to an eligible unopened change-of-mind return when the order's promotional terms explicitly state it. The customer should check the order confirmation.

## 8. Missing information
This policy does not specify whether a return can be extended beyond 14 days for personal circumstances such as travel, examinations, or holidays. Such exceptions require human support approval and must not be invented by the assistant.

## 21. Common customer phrasing
Customers may describe E-03 without using the code. Phrases such as "three beeps", "beeps three times", "red light after the beeps", or "filter alarm" should be mapped through semantic retrieval to the documented E-03 content. Retrieval should still verify the product line because other products can have different indicators.

## 22. Model identification
Before applying model-specific instructions, identify the product line from the customer's wording or ask a clarifying question when necessary. The assistant must not silently apply AquaPearl 500 Pro procedures to AeroPearl Mini or a discontinued model.

## 23. Price interpretation
Pricing answers should identify the SKU and distinguish list price from current promotional price. If multiple filters match the word "filter", the assistant should list the relevant options or ask which product line the customer owns.

## 24. Policy interpretation
Policy answers should quote or paraphrase only the applicable rule. If a question combines return, warranty, delivery, and service terms, retrieval may need several sources. The assistant should separate each rule instead of blending them into a new policy.

## 25. Unsupported commitments
The system must never invent a phone number, discount percentage, refund amount, delivery exception, certification, or service booking. When the knowledge base is silent, the response should use the documented escalation route.

## 26. Evaluation examples
A good evaluation question tests a known fact, a cross-document seam, a follow-up, an unanswerable gap, an ambiguity, or an adversarial instruction. The expected source should be identified before running the model so retrieval can be scored independently.

## 27. Human verification
Source citations are intended to let a support agent inspect the evidence. A citation is not permission for the model to make a claim that the cited passage does not support.
