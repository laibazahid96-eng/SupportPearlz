# Pearlz Home Systems Warranty Policy
Version: 2026.2
Last updated: 2026-06-01
Doc type: policy
Product lines: AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, AeroPearl Mini

## 1. Warranty periods
AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, and AeroPearl Mini carry a standard limited warranty of 24 months from the invoice date. The invoice date is the starting point; the delivery date does not extend the warranty. Discontinued AquaPearl 100 and AeroPearl Classic units sold before discontinuation retain the extended warranty period printed on their original warranty certificate, subject to the exclusions in this policy.

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship during normal residential use. The covered purifier body, pump, electronics, and factory-installed motor are eligible for repair or replacement at Pearlz's discretion.

## 2. What is covered
The purifier housing, internal pump, control electronics, factory-installed motor, and workmanship defects are covered when the failure is attributable to a manufacturing defect. A replacement part supplied during a warranty repair inherits the remainder of the original product warranty; it does not create a new 24-month period.

## 3. Exclusions
Filter cartridges and other consumables are excluded. Physical damage, cracked housings caused by impact, damage caused by unauthorised repair or modification, commercial use of a residential product, and damage attributable to water quality outside the published operating range are excluded.

A warranty period alone does not override an exclusion. For example, a pump failure 14 months after purchase remains within the 24-month period, but a failure following an unauthorised repair is not covered by the standard warranty. Customers may request paid service in such cases.

Property damage is not automatically covered merely because the purifier itself is covered. The policy does not authorise compensation for cabinets, countertops, flooring, or other third-party property unless a separate written settlement has been approved by Pearlz.

## 4. Resale and transfer
The warranty may be transferred once to a subsequent residential owner if the original invoice and transfer details are supplied. The remaining period transfers; it does not restart on resale.

## 5. How to start a warranty claim
A customer should provide the purchase invoice, product serial number, a description of the fault, and photographs when physical damage is relevant. Support may request diagnostic information. The service agreement contains the detailed scheduling and technician procedure for warranty claims.

## 6. Extended warranty
Discontinued models remain eligible only for the extended terms shown on their original certificate. The current standard 24-month period for active products must not be applied to a discontinued model without checking its certificate.

## 7. Important interpretation rule
If two clauses appear relevant, apply both. A customer must satisfy the time requirement and must not fall under an exclusion. This document does not promise refunds, credits, exceptions, or property-damage compensation beyond the terms explicitly written here.

## 22. Model identification
Before applying model-specific instructions, identify the product line from the customer's wording or ask a clarifying question when necessary. The assistant must not silently apply AquaPearl 500 Pro procedures to AeroPearl Mini or a discontinued model.

## 23. Price interpretation
Pricing answers should identify the SKU and distinguish list price from current promotional price. If multiple filters match the word "filter", the assistant should list the relevant options or ask which product line the customer owns.

## 24. Policy interpretation
Policy answers should quote or paraphrase only the applicable rule. If a question combines return, warranty, delivery, and service terms, retrieval may need several sources. The assistant should separate each rule instead of blending them into a new policy.

## 25. Unsupported commitments
The system must never invent a phone number, discount percentage, refund amount, delivery exception, certification, or service booking. When the knowledge base is silent, the response should use the documented escalation route.

## 26. Evaluation examples
A good evaluation question tests a known fact, a cross-document seam, a follow-up, an unanswerable gap, an ambiguity, or an adversarial instruction. The expected source should be identified before running the model so retrieval can be scored independently.

## 27. Human verification
Source citations are intended to let a support agent inspect the evidence. A citation is not permission for the model to make a claim that the cited passage does not support.
