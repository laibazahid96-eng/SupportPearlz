# Pearlz Privacy Policy
Version: 2026.1
Last updated: 2026-04-20
Doc type: policy
Product line: all

## 1. Information collected
Pearlz collects account information such as name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details when customers purchase products. Payment credentials are handled by payment processors; Pearlz does not store full payment-card numbers in its customer support system.

## 2. Mobile app data
When a customer connects a supported smart purifier to the Pearlz mobile application, the app may collect device identifiers, connectivity status, filter-status information, usage readings, and water-usage telemetry. The app uses this information to display device status and maintenance notifications.

## 3. Order and support information
Customer service interactions can include ticket content, product serial numbers, order references, photographs, and troubleshooting details supplied by the customer. Agents use this information to resolve support requests.

## 4. Retention
Order records are retained for the period required for accounting, warranty, service, and legal obligations. Support tickets are retained for operational quality and dispute handling. The policy does not promise deletion on an exact number of days for every category.

## 5. Third parties
Pearlz may share necessary information with courier providers for delivery, payment processors for transaction handling, and contracted service providers when required to perform a support or installation service. Third parties are expected to handle information according to contractual obligations.

## 6. User rights
Customers may request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where legally available, or export of eligible data. Privacy requests should be directed to the privacy contact listed in the customer's account materials.

## 7. Cookies
The online store uses cookies and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, analytics, and site functionality. Disabling some cookies can affect site features.

## 8. Security
Pearlz uses access controls and operational safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Customers should not send passwords or payment-card secrets through support channels.

## 9. App telemetry choice
The policy does not state that every category of water-usage telemetry can be disabled independently. Do not promise a specific opt-out control unless documented for the customer's app version.

## 10. Scope
This document describes Pearlz's fictional support environment for the SupportPearlz prototype. It does not establish legal advice for a real company.

## 11. Data minimisation
Pearlz support workflows should request only information needed to resolve the issue. A support agent should not ask for a password or full card number. Product serial numbers and order references can be appropriate when verifying a service request.

## 12. Photographs
Customers may provide photographs of packaging, physical damage, product labels, or installation conditions when relevant. Images should be retained only according to the applicable operational retention process.

## 13. Access requests
A request to access personal data is different from a normal product-support question. It should be routed to the privacy process rather than answered by guessing what records exist.

## 14. Deletion requests
Deletion rights can be subject to legal and operational exceptions. The policy therefore does not promise that every record will be deleted immediately on request.

## 15. Export
Eligible data may be available through an export request. The policy does not specify an exact file format or turnaround time for every request.

## 16. Analytics
Cookies and analytics technologies can support site operations and measurement. The policy does not identify every vendor or every individual cookie name.

## 17. Telemetry interpretation
Water-usage telemetry is operational information collected by supported app functionality. The assistant should not infer medical, health, or environmental conclusions from telemetry values.

## 18. Third-party processors
Couriers and payment processors may receive information necessary to perform their services. The privacy policy does not grant those providers permission to use customer information for unrelated purposes beyond their contractual arrangements.

## 19. Security incidents
The policy states that safeguards are used but does not provide a public incident-response playbook. Customers reporting suspected account compromise should be routed to the appropriate human security process.

## 20. Privacy and support
A customer can ask a support question without being required to disclose unnecessary sensitive information. When a question needs account verification, the agent should request only the minimum appropriate identifier.
