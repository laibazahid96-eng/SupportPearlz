## 1. Product overview
The AquaPearl 500 Pro is a residential smart water purifier designed for treated source water within the published operating range. It combines staged filtration, a monitored storage tank, electronic controls, and maintenance alerts. This manual describes ordinary operation, maintenance, and safety boundaries.

The standard package contains the AquaPearl 500 Pro purifier body, mounting bracket, mounting hardware, inlet tubing, drain accessories where applicable, a filter set, power adapter, quick-start guide, warranty information, and an installation template. ## 4. Control panel
The control panel contains POWER, FILTER, ALERT, and RESET controls. Indicator lights provide status information. A steady green power indicator means the unit has electrical power and is in a normal powered state. A maintenance indicator can illuminate when filter service is due. A red alert indicator means the unit has detected a condition requiring attention.

Do not interpret a light color in isolation when an alert code is displayed. Use the alert-code table and current troubleshooting guide.

## 5. Normal operation
Open the water supply fully enough to provide the required inlet pressure. Power on the purifier and allow the normal startup sequence to complete. The unit may run briefly to fill internal passages and stabilise pressure. During normal operation, the system can cycle according to demand.

Do not block ventilation openings. Do not place heavy objects on the purifier or use tubing as a support. Keep the unit level and accessible for maintenance.

## 6. Filter stages
The sediment stage captures suspended particles. The carbon stage supports reduction of taste- and odour-affecting compounds. The membrane stage provides the primary fine filtration step in the configured system. The polishing stage provides final conditioning according to the supplied filter set.

Filter replacement must follow the product's maintenance indicator and the replacement schedule. Use only compatible cartridges identified for the AquaPearl 500 Pro.

## 7. Filter replacement
Before replacing a cartridge, close the inlet water supply and follow the shutdown procedure. Remove the spent cartridge without forcing fittings. Install the matching replacement cartridge in the correct position. Re-open the supply slowly and inspect for leaks.

After replacement, complete the first-run flushing procedure described in the Installation Guide. Do not consume the initial water before the flush has been completed.

## 8. Maintenance schedule
Inspect the unit for leaks during routine household checks. Wipe the exterior with a soft damp cloth. Do not spray cleaning chemicals directly into openings. Follow the indicator-driven filter replacement schedule.

A periodic descaling or cleaning cycle should be performed at the interval stated by the current service guidance and according to local source-water conditions. Customers should not dismantle internal pump or electrical assemblies to perform routine cleaning.

## 9. Cleaning
Disconnect power when the cleaning procedure requires access to a service area. Use a soft cloth for external surfaces. Do not immerse the electrical components. Do not use abrasive powders or solvents on the housing.

When a descaling procedure is specified, use only the approved method and solution described by Pearlz service documentation. If the unit shows an electrical fault during cleaning, stop and escalate.

## 10. Audible alert codes
E-01 — inlet-water interruption. Check the water supply and inlet connection.
E-02 — flow sensor anomaly. Check for an unusual flow condition and contact service if persistent.
E-03 — sediment pre-filter saturation. Replace the sediment pre-filter and hold RESET for five seconds.
E-04 — pump protection. Stop repeated operation and contact support if the condition persists.
E-05 — over-temperature condition. Allow the unit to cool and investigate the installation environment.
E-06 — communication fault. Follow current app and troubleshooting guidance; firmware 2.4 changed its diagnostic wording.
E-07 — tank-level sensor anomaly. Check normal operation and contact service if persistent.
E-08 — service-required condition. Contact trained service personnel.

Three short beeps on the AquaPearl 500 Pro correspond to E-03. The three-beep sound should not be interpreted as a general-purpose alarm without checking the alert table.

## 11. Indicator combinations
A blinking red alert indicator normally accompanies an active alert condition. If the red indicator is combined with a displayed code, use the code as the primary diagnostic identifier.

If an indicator appears to conflict with a written status message, do not bypass the warning. Record the code, turn off the unit if the troubleshooting guide calls for it, and contact support.

## 12. Resetting alerts
The reset control should be used only for the alert conditions for which a reset sequence is documented. For E-03, the sequence is performed after the sediment pre-filter is replaced: press and hold RESET for five seconds.

Repeatedly resetting an alert without addressing its cause can hide a maintenance condition. If E-03 returns immediately after a correct replacement, verify the cartridge SKU and escalate if the alert remains.

## 13. Water quality
The purifier is intended for source water within the published operating conditions. Source-water quality can influence filter life, flow, and taste. A high TDS reading is not by itself proof that a particular filter is defective.

If water quality is unusual, the customer should follow the troubleshooting process and seek appropriate water-quality advice. The assistant must not make health or safety claims beyond the product documentation.

## 14. Leak response
If a visible external connection leaks, stop water flow and inspect the accessible connection. If a leak continues, contact support. If water is present near electrical components, do not continue operation.

A cracked housing caused by impact is physical damage and is excluded from the standard warranty. The product should be assessed by service personnel rather than structurally repaired by the customer.

## 15. Pump noise
A small change in pump sound can occur during normal cycling. Persistent loud noise can indicate air in the line, inlet restrictions, or a pump condition. Check installation connections without opening the pump. If the noise persists, stop use as advised by troubleshooting and contact support.

## 16. Mobile app
The supported mobile application can display device status, maintenance information, and connectivity state. Pairing problems should be handled through the app's current pairing process. Keep the app updated.

The privacy policy explains that supported app use may involve device identifiers, connectivity information, filter status, and water-usage telemetry.

## 17. Installation boundary
The product must be installed according to the Installation Guide. Water pressure outside the specified 20–80 PSI range can affect operation. Electrical connections must use the appropriate grounded supply.

Self-installation is permitted when performed correctly. Damage caused by incorrect installation, modification, or use outside requirements can affect warranty assessment.

## 18. Storage and transport
Keep the packaged unit dry and protected from freezing temperatures. Do not place heavy loads on the carton. After transport, allow the unit to reach the installation environment before connecting if condensation is present.

## 19. Safety warnings
Do not bypass safety switches. Do not repair the circuit board. Do not open electrical compartments unless you are trained and authorised to do so. Disconnect power before service procedures that require it.

Smoke, burning smells, exposed wiring, or severe leaks near electrical parts require immediate shutdown and trained service escalation.

## 20. Troubleshooting quick reference
For no power, verify the socket and power connection. For low flow, check filter condition and inlet pressure. For bad taste immediately after a filter change, complete the first-run flush. For three beeps, address E-03. For persistent leaks or electrical concerns, stop and escalate.

## 21. Cleaning and descaling rationale
Routine cleaning helps maintain external hygiene and service access. Descaling may be needed where source water produces mineral deposits. The customer should not improvise chemicals or open sealed internal assemblies.
