# Planted Gaps and Cross-Document Seams

## Deliberate seams
1. **Warranty → Service Agreement:** the warranty policy identifies required claim evidence and refers detailed scheduling/technician procedure to the service agreement.
2. **Troubleshooting → Installation:** troubleshooting identifies bad taste after filter replacement, while the first-run flushing procedure is documented in the installation guide.
3. **Shipping → Customer Service:** shipping defines normal delivery windows but does not authorise special delivery exceptions; the handbook controls escalation for special commitments.

## Deliberate gaps
1. Student discounts are not documented.
2. NSF certification of filters is not established.
3. Instalment-payment plans are not documented.

## Additional negative cases
International shipping, exact post-dispatch cancellation rules, live courier tracking, and arbitrary property-damage compensation are not fully documented. The assistant must not invent answers for these gaps.
