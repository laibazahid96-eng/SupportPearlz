# Service and Maintenance Agreement
Version: 2026.2
Last updated: 2026-05-30
Doc type: agreement
Product lines: AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, AeroPearl Mini

## 1. Service tiers
Pearlz offers three fictional annual maintenance tiers: Care Basic, Care Plus, and Care Premium. The current service schedule determines pricing.

Care Basic includes one preventive visit per year and standard remote support. Care Plus includes two preventive visits per year and priority scheduling. Care Premium includes quarterly preventive visits, priority scheduling, and expanded diagnostic support.

## 2. Response-time targets
Care Basic targets a service response within 3 working days. Care Plus targets 2 working days. Care Premium targets 1 working day. These are service targets, not guarantees of completed repair within those periods.

## 3. Parts
Consumable filters are billed separately unless a specific service package says otherwise. Covered warranty parts are handled under the warranty policy. Non-warranty replacement parts are billed to the customer after approval.

## 4. Warranty claim procedure
When a warranty policy directs a customer to the service agreement, the claim procedure is: verify the invoice and serial number; record the fault; perform remote diagnostics when appropriate; create a service ticket; schedule a technician if required; and document the outcome. The service technician records replaced parts and the reason for replacement.

This claim procedure is intentionally located in this agreement rather than the warranty policy.

## 5. Cancellation
Annual maintenance plans can be cancelled with written notice. Any pro-rata refund is calculated according to unused service periods and completed visits. The exact refund amount is not guaranteed in this document without reviewing the customer's plan and visit history.

## 6. Service safety
Technicians must follow electrical and water-isolation procedures. Customers should not interfere with a technician's safety controls.

## 7. Commercial commitments
The agreement does not authorise support agents or the AI assistant to promise credits, exceptions, refunds, or service upgrades outside approved terms.

## 8. Discontinued products
Service availability for discontinued models depends on parts availability and the terms of their extended warranty. A standard active-product service promise should not automatically be applied to a discontinued product.

## 9. Visit preparation
Before a technician visit, the customer should have the product accessible and retain the invoice and serial number. If the issue is intermittent, the customer can record the displayed alert code or a short description of when it occurs. Technicians should document diagnostics rather than rely on an unverified customer conclusion.

## 10. Parts approval
For non-warranty service, the technician identifies the required part and the customer receives the applicable charge for approval. The assistant must not invent a part price when the pricing guide does not contain that part.

## 11. Preventive maintenance
Preventive visits can include inspection of connections, filter-status review, operating checks, and service recommendations. The exact activities depend on the tier and product. Preventive maintenance does not automatically extend the manufacturer's warranty.

## 12. SLA interpretation
A response-time SLA describes the target time for service response. It does not guarantee that a repair will be completed within the same interval. Customers should not be told that an SLA is a completion guarantee.

## 13. Warranty coordination
When a service ticket may be covered by warranty, the service team verifies the invoice date and exclusions. A ticket can be opened before final coverage determination. Coverage is decided under the Warranty Policy.

## 14. Cancellation examples
If a customer cancels after one of two scheduled visits has already been completed, the pro-rata calculation considers the completed visit and the unused period. The agreement does not contain a universal fixed refund percentage for every plan.

## 15. Customer communication
Technicians should explain what was tested, what was changed, and what the customer should monitor. They should avoid promising future policy exceptions. If a customer disputes the outcome, the issue is escalated through the support process.

## 16. Records
Service records may contain serial numbers, fault descriptions, replaced parts, technician notes, and scheduling information. These records are operational data and should be handled according to Pearlz privacy and access controls.

## 17. Emergency boundary
The maintenance agreement does not replace emergency safety procedures. Smoke, burning smells, exposed wiring, or severe electrical hazards should be escalated immediately according to the safety notes.

## 18. AI boundary
The SupportPearlz assistant may explain the documented service process, but it cannot create a service ticket unless a real service tool is connected. It must not claim that a technician has been booked merely because a customer asked.
