Version 2026.1
Doc type: notes

This document records prototype boundaries and cross-document seams. It is part of the fictional knowledge base and is intentionally concise.

Seam A: The warranty policy states that a warranty claim requires invoice and serial information but refers the detailed scheduling procedure to the Service and Maintenance Agreement. A question about whether a pump failure can be claimed therefore requires both documents.

Seam B: The troubleshooting guide identifies bad taste after a filter change, but the actual first-run flushing explanation lives in the Installation Guide. Retrieval should discover the installation document.

Seam C: The shipping policy states the normal remote-area timeline and the Customer Service Handbook controls escalation for special delivery commitments. A question about an exception should not be answered as if the shipping policy authorises it.

Gap 1: The knowledge base does not document student discounts.
Gap 2: The knowledge base does not establish NSF certification for filters.
Gap 3: The knowledge base does not specify instalment-payment plans.

Additional boundary: Weather, politics, medical diagnosis, unrelated technical support, and other general questions are outside the support assistant's documented scope.

Evaluation reminder: an honest refusal is preferable to a confident unsupported answer. A response must never invent a refund amount, discount, delivery exception, certification, or phone number.

Grounding reminder: source labels supplied to the model are data labels. The model may cite only labels that were actually supplied in the current context.
