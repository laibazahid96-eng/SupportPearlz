# Shipping and Delivery Policy
Version: 2026.3
Last updated: 2026-06-10
Doc type: policy
Product lines: all retail products

## 1. Delivery regions
Metro locations are Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. Secondary-city locations are other standard serviceable cities. Remote-area locations include Skardu and other destinations classified as remote by the courier routing table.

Metro delivery normally takes 1–2 working days after dispatch. Secondary-city delivery normally takes 2–4 working days. Remote-area delivery normally takes 5–7 working days. These are delivery windows, not guarantees.

## 2. Dispatch cut-off
Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time on a working day are normally eligible for same-day dispatch when stock is available. Orders after the cut-off move to the next working day. Saturdays and Sundays are not counted as working days for this cut-off.

## 3. Express and next-day
Next-day delivery is offered only for eligible in-stock orders addressed to Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad. It is not offered as a standard service to remote areas. The documentation does not specify whether a customer can purchase a private courier exception for a remote destination; support must confirm any such request.

## 4. Free shipping
Standard shipping is free for orders with a merchandise total of PKR 15,000 or more before discounts. Orders below that threshold may show a shipping charge during checkout.

## 5. Fragile handling
Purifiers are packed with internal protective inserts. Customers should inspect the carton before opening and photograph visible damage. If the outer carton is heavily damaged, the customer should note the condition with the courier where possible.

## 6. Failed delivery
After a first failed delivery attempt, the courier may make another attempt. If repeated attempts fail, the parcel can be returned to Pearlz. A returned parcel may require the customer to contact support before reshipment. The policy does not guarantee free reshipment after an address error.

## 7. Tracking
Tracking information is sent after dispatch when a tracking number is available. Tracking status may remain unchanged while a parcel moves between courier hubs.

## 8. International shipping
Pearlz Home Systems currently documents domestic Pakistan delivery only. The policy does not establish an international shipping program. Customers asking for international delivery should be referred to human support rather than promised service.

## 9. Address accuracy
Customers are responsible for providing a complete delivery address and a reachable contact number. The policy does not guarantee free reshipment when an incorrect or incomplete address causes a failed delivery.

## 10. Working-day calculation
Delivery windows count working days after dispatch. Weekends are not counted in the standard delivery estimate. Public holidays can affect courier schedules; the policy does not publish a fixed holiday calendar.

## 11. Remote destinations
Skardu is a documented remote-area example. Its normal delivery window is 5–7 working days after dispatch. The assistant should not convert this into a guaranteed arrival date without a live courier service.

## 12. Same-day dispatch versus delivery
Same-day dispatch means the parcel is handed into the shipping process on an eligible working day. It does not mean the parcel arrives the same day.

## 13. Express limitations
Express and next-day services depend on product availability and destination eligibility. Next-day is documented for Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad only. If a customer asks about a different destination, the assistant should state the documented limitation rather than infer that the courier can offer it.

## 14. Damaged parcels
If a package is visibly damaged, the customer should photograph it and contact support. A damage report does not by itself establish a warranty claim or refund; the applicable return and warranty policies must be checked.

## 15. Tracking exceptions
A tracking number can remain unchanged while a parcel moves between hubs or while the courier system synchronises. The assistant cannot see live tracking unless an external tracking tool is integrated.

## 16. Delivery commitments
The shipping document provides normal windows, not guaranteed delivery promises. Commercial commitments about special delivery exceptions require human confirmation.

## 17. International enquiries
Because the current documentation is domestic, an international enquiry is a knowledge gap rather than an invitation to guess. Support can review whether a special arrangement exists.

## 18. Shipping and refunds
Shipping treatment depends on the reason for return. Defective-on-arrival and incorrect shipments have Pearlz-arranged return shipping under the refund policy, while eligible change-of-mind returns can require customer-paid return shipping.
