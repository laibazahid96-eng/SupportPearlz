# AquaPearl Installation Guide
Version: 2026.2
Last updated: 2026-05-28
Doc type: guide
Product line: AquaPearl 500 Pro

## 1. Before installation
Confirm a clean installation location, a stable wall or under-sink mounting surface, a grounded 220–240 V socket, and water pressure between 20 and 80 PSI. Keep at least 100 mm clearance around ventilation openings and allow enough space to remove filter cartridges.

Do not install the purifier where it will be exposed to freezing temperatures, direct heat, or persistent standing water. Turn off the water supply before making plumbing connections.

## 2. Wall mounting
Mark the mounting bracket holes using the supplied template. Use fasteners appropriate to the wall material. The unit must be level and securely supported. Do not hang the purifier from a pipe or flexible hose.

Connect the inlet line to the marked inlet port and confirm the flow direction. Open the water supply slowly and inspect every connection for leaks before powering the unit.

## 3. Under-sink installation
Place the unit so the filter access area remains reachable. Avoid sharp bends in tubing. Keep electrical connections dry and away from plumbing joints. The drain or reject-water line, where applicable, must not be kinked.

## 4. First-run flushing
After installation or replacement of a filter set, flush the system before drinking the water. Run the specified flush cycle until the initial water clears and the unit's first-run indicator completes. The first water can contain harmless carbon fines and manufacturing residue, so it should not be consumed.

A customer who reports an unusual taste immediately after changing a filter should first verify that the required first-run flush was completed.

## 5. Professional installation
Professional installation is available for a service fee shown at checkout or in the current service schedule. A professional visit includes connection checks and a first-run verification.

## 6. Self-installation and warranty
Incorrect self-installation can cause damage. Warranty coverage is not automatically voided merely because a customer installs the product, but damage caused by incorrect installation, modification, or use outside the installation requirements can be excluded.

## 7. Safety
Disconnect electrical power before opening service-access panels. Customers must not bypass safety switches or modify wiring. Electrical faults, persistent water leaks inside the electrical compartment, or signs of burning require escalation to trained service personnel.

Phrases such as "three beeps", "beeps three times", "red light after the beeps", or "filter alarm" should be mapped through semantic retrieval to the documented E-03 content. ## 22. Model identification
Before applying model-specific instructions, identify the product line from the customer's wording or ask a clarifying question when necessary. The assistant must not silently apply AquaPearl 500 Pro procedures to AeroPearl Mini or a discontinued model.

## 23. Price interpretation
Pricing answers should identify the SKU and distinguish list price from current promotional price. If multiple filters match the word "filter", the assistant should list the relevant options or ask which product line the customer owns.

## 24. Policy interpretation
Policy answers should quote or paraphrase only the applicable rule. If a question combines return, warranty, delivery, and service terms, retrieval may need several sources. The assistant should separate each rule instead of blending them into a new policy.

## 25. Unsupported commitments
The system must never invent a phone number, discount percentage, refund amount, delivery exception, certification, or service booking. When the knowledge base is silent, the response should use the documented escalation route.

## 26. Evaluation examples
A good evaluation question tests a known fact, a cross-document seam, a follow-up, an unanswerable gap, an ambiguity, or an adversarial instruction. The expected source should be identified before running the model so retrieval can be scored independently.

## 27. Human verification
