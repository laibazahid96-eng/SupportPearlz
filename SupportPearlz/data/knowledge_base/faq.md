# Pearlz Customer FAQ
Version: 2026.2
Last updated: 2026-06-05
Doc type: faq
Product lines: all

## Ordering and products
### 1. Which purifier is currently sold?
The active lines are AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, and AeroPearl Mini. AquaPearl 100 and AeroPearl Classic are discontinued but may remain under extended warranty.

### 2. How long is the AquaPearl 500 Pro warranty?
The standard warranty is 24 months from the invoice date, subject to exclusions.

### 3. What is in the AquaPearl 500 Pro box?
The box contains the purifier, standard mounting hardware, inlet tubing, drain accessories where applicable, filter set, power adapter, quick-start material, and installation template. Exact packaging can change; use the current product manual for the authoritative list.

### 4. How much is the filter?
There are multiple replacement filters. The correct price depends on the SKU and product line. Ask which filter or use the pricing guide rather than assuming one price.

### 5. Can I buy an AeroPearl Mini filter for an AquaPearl?
Only a filter explicitly listed as compatible with the product line should be used. The pricing guide identifies compatibility.

### 6. Can I install the purifier myself?
Yes, provided the installation requirements are followed. Incorrect installation can cause damage that is excluded from warranty coverage.

### 7. Why does new water taste strange?
After a filter replacement, complete the required first-run flushing cycle. Carbon fines and manufacturing residue can affect the first water.

### 8. What does three beeps mean?
Three short beeps indicate E-03, the sediment pre-filter saturation alert, on the AquaPearl 500 Pro.

### 9. Can I reset E-03?
Replace the sediment pre-filter and then hold RESET for five seconds.

### 10. Can I return an opened purifier?
An opened product can be returned within 14 days when it is defective on arrival or materially different from the ordered item. A change-of-mind return after opening is not a standard eligible return.

### 11. How long do refunds take?
Pearlz processing can take up to 5 business days for card payments after inspection and approval. Provider settlement time is additional.

### 12. Does Pearlz ship to Skardu?
Skardu is classified as a remote-area destination and the normal delivery window is 5–7 working days after dispatch.

### 13. Is next-day delivery available to Skardu?
The documented next-day service is limited to eligible orders in Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad. The documentation does not promise next-day service to Skardu.

### 14. What is free shipping?
Standard shipping is free when merchandise totals PKR 15,000 or more before discounts.

### 15. What documents are needed for warranty?
The purchase invoice and product serial number are required, with fault details and photographs when relevant.

### 16. Does the warranty cover filters?
Filter cartridges are consumables and are excluded from the standard warranty.

### 17. Does warranty cover a cracked housing?
Physical damage, including impact-related cracked housings, is excluded from the standard warranty.

### 18. Can I transfer the warranty?
The warranty may be transferred once to a subsequent residential owner if the original invoice and transfer details are supplied.

### 19. What if the pump fails after an unauthorised repair?
The failure can fall under the unauthorised-repair exclusion even if the product is still within the 24-month period. Paid service may be available.

### 20. What are support hours?
Customer Service support hours and escalation rules are in the Customer Service Handbook.

### 21. Do you offer student discounts?
The current knowledge base does not document a student discount. Support should confirm any special offer.

### 22. Is the filter NSF-certified?
The knowledge base does not establish an NSF certification claim. Do not infer certification from filtration performance.

### 23. Can I pay in instalments?
The knowledge base does not specify an instalment-payment program.

### 24. Can you compensate me for my kitchen cabinet?
The warranty policy does not automatically authorise property-damage compensation. Human support must review any such claim.

### 25. Can I get international delivery?
The current shipping documentation covers domestic Pakistan delivery only and does not establish an international service.

### 26. How often should filters be changed?
Follow the product manual's filter-status indicators and replacement schedule for the specific model.

### 27. Can I use a third-party cartridge?
Use only compatible cartridges identified for the product. Using an unsuitable cartridge can cause performance problems and may affect warranty assessment.

### 28. What if the courier loses my parcel?
Tracking and failed-delivery handling are described in the shipping policy. A specific lost-parcel resolution requires support review.

### 29. Can I cancel after dispatch?
The knowledge base does not define a universal post-dispatch cancellation rule. Ask support.

### 30. What if my order arrives damaged?
Photograph the packaging and product and contact support. Defective-on-arrival or incorrect shipments are eligible for return shipping arranged by Pearlz.

### 31. Is professional installation available?
Yes. Professional installation is available for a service fee shown through the current service schedule.

### 32. Can I repair the pump myself?
No. Customers should not disassemble the pump. Persistent faults require trained service personnel.

### 33. What does E-06 mean?
E-06 is a communication fault. Firmware 2.4 changed the wording of this diagnostic but did not change E-03.

### 34. What if my unit keeps running?
Continuous running can indicate a flow sensor or inlet condition. Check the inlet and contact support if the problem persists.

### 35. Can I return a filter cartridge?
Used consumable filter cartridges are non-refundable.

### 36. What if I only have the delivery date, not the invoice date?
Warranty duration is calculated from the invoice date, not the delivery date. Support may need the invoice to confirm coverage.

## 21. Common customer phrasing
Customers may describe E-03 without using the code. Phrases such as "three beeps", "beeps three times", "red light after the beeps", or "filter alarm" should be mapped through semantic retrieval to the documented E-03 content. Retrieval should still verify the product line because other products can have different indicators.

## 22. Model identification
Before applying model-specific instructions, identify the product line from the customer's wording or ask a clarifying question when necessary. The assistant must not silently apply AquaPearl 500 Pro procedures to AeroPearl Mini or a discontinued model.

## 23. Price interpretation
Pricing answers should identify the SKU and distinguish list price from current promotional price. If multiple filters match the word "filter", the assistant should list the relevant options or ask which product line the customer owns.

## 24. Policy interpretation
Policy answers should quote or paraphrase only the applicable rule. If a question combines return, warranty, delivery, and service terms, retrieval may need several sources. The assistant should separate each rule instead of blending them into a new policy.

## 25. Unsupported commitments
The system must never invent a phone number, discount percentage, refund amount, delivery exception, certification, or service booking. When the knowledge base is silent, the response should use the documented escalation route.

## 26. Evaluation examples
A good evaluation question tests a known fact, a cross-document seam, a follow-up, an unanswerable gap, an ambiguity, or an adversarial instruction. The expected source should be identified before running the model so retrieval can be scored independently.

## 27. Human verification
Source citations are intended to let a support agent inspect the evidence. A citation is not permission for the model to make a claim that the cited passage does not support.

## 28. Version control
When a policy or manual changes, its version and last-updated fields should change. Old chunks should be removed or superseded during re-indexing. Keeping both versions active without a version preference can produce contradictory answers.

## 29. Operational limitation
This academic prototype has no live order-management, payment, courier, or service-booking integration. It can explain documentation but cannot inspect a real order or execute a refund.

## 30. Final support principle
If evidence is strong, answer concisely and cite it. If evidence is partial, answer the supported portion and identify the gap. If evidence is absent or the question is outside scope, refuse plainly and direct the customer to human support.
