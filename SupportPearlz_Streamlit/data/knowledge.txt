SupportPearlz Customer Support Knowledge Base

WARRANTY:
Pearlz Home Systems products have a standard 2-year limited warranty from the purchase date.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical damage caused by misuse is not covered.

RETURNS:
Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery.
The product should normally be unused and in its original packaging.
Refunds are processed after the returned product is inspected.

SHIPPING:
Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping normally takes 1 to 2 business days.

INSTALLATION:
Customers should read the product installation guide before installation.
For electrical installation, use a qualified technician.

SUPPORT:
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Support can help with product setup and troubleshooting.

SAFETY:
Do not open electrical components while the product is connected to power.
Disconnect power before cleaning or maintenance.
